Why BakerIN

Fresh is not a slogan here. It is the schedule.

Small batches, short shelf life, honest ingredients. If it is not good enough for our own table, it does not leave the kitchen.

Baked fresh, every single morning

Nothing is frozen or carried over. Batches are sized to the day, so what you buy was made hours ago — not weeks.

Ingredients you can pronounce

Belgian couverture, cultured butter, seasonal fruit and single-origin cocoa. No hydrogenated fats, no artificial preservatives.

Made to order, not made in advance

Custom cakes are baked, filled and finished for your date and time slot. Eggless, low-sugar and nut-free variants on request.

An FSSAI-licensed, hygiene-first kitchen

Temperature-controlled storage, daily sanitation logs and sealed packaging — the same standards we want for our own family.
