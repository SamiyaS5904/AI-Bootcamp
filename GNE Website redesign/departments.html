Departments

Ten departments run six undergraduate engineering branches, a five-year architecture programme, three management and computing degrees, and eleven postgraduate programmes — all under UGC autonomous status, so syllabi and evaluation are set in-house.

B.Arch.

School of Architecture

A five-year professional programme taught through design studios, with building construction, history of architecture, urban planning and professional practice alongside the studio sequence.

Programmes
B.Arch. (5 years)
Focus areas
Architectural design, urban design, sustainable building

Applied Science

Physics, chemistry, mathematics and communication skills for the first two years of every engineering branch, plus research supervision in applied physics and materials chemistry.

Programmes
Service teaching across all UG branches; Ph.D.
Focus areas
Applied physics, materials chemistry, applied mathematics
Labs
Physics, chemistry and environmental science laboratories

B.Tech. · M.Tech. · Ph.D.

Civil Engineering

One of the founding departments. Teaching and consultancy span structures, geotechnical engineering, transportation, water resources and environmental engineering, with a testing cell that serves regional industry and government bodies.

Programmes
B.Tech.; B.Tech. for working professionals; M.Tech.; Ph.D.
Focus areas
Structures, geotechnics, transportation, environmental

B.Tech. · M.Tech. · Ph.D.

Electrical Engineering

Power systems, electrical machines, drives, control and renewable energy integration. The department's alumni run large parts of Punjab's power utilities.

Programmes
B.Tech.; M.Tech.; Ph.D.
Focus areas
Power systems, machines and drives, renewable energy
Notable alumni
Air Marshal R. K. S. Shera; Er. A. S. Chatha, IAS

B.Tech. · M.Tech. · Ph.D.

Mechanical & Production Engineering

Design, thermal engineering, manufacturing processes and industrial automation, backed by the college workshops. The department hosts the long-running international conference on advancements and futuristic trends in mechanical and materials engineering.

Programmes
B.Tech.; B.Tech. for working professionals; M.Tech.; Ph.D.
Focus areas
Design, thermal, manufacturing, materials, automation
Working professionals
99142 55011

B.Tech. · M.Tech. · Ph.D.

Electronics & Communication Engineering

VLSI design, embedded systems, signal and image processing, optical and wireless communication. Alumni include the managing director of IBM India and the vice-chancellor of IKGPTU.

Programmes
B.Tech.; M.Tech.; Ph.D.
Focus areas
VLSI, embedded systems, signal processing, communication
Notable alumni
Er. Karan Bajwa; Dr. Ajay Kumar Sharma
