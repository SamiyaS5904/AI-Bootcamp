About GNDEC

A trust-run college with the freedom of an autonomous institution.

Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College grew out of the polytechnic established in Ludhiana in 1953 and was founded in its own right in 1956 under the Nankana Sahib Education Trust. It is approved by AICTE, affiliated to I. K. Gujral Punjab Technical University, and has held UGC autonomous status since 2012 — so curriculum, examination and evaluation are designed in-house rather than inherited.

Competitive funding includes Rs 10 crore under TEQIP-II and Rs 1 crore under the DST FIST programme.

12th International Conference on Advancements and Futuristic Trends in Mechanical and Materials Engineering

The department’s long-running international conference brings researchers and industry together on materials, manufacturing and design. Call for papers and registration are open — details on the notice board.

Addresses by prominent personalities

A standing series that brings senior figures from industry, government and research onto campus. Sessions are held in the main block and are open to visitors as well as students.

NSS and NCC on campus

Around 350 enrolled NSS volunteers, with more than a thousand students taking part across a year, alongside an NCC unit of 106 cadets. Both run camps, drives and community work through the session.
