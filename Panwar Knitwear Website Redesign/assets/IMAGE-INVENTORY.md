## ✅ USABLE — 29 real product photographs

**Dimensions:** 982 × 1147 px (portrait, ~0.86 ratio) · **Format:** JPG · **Size:** 100–560 KB each

**These are the backbone of the site.** Every one is a real Panwar Knitwear product shot, and they are far more useful than they first appear:

- Each frame shows a **hero garment laid flat** plus a **stacked colour run** beside it — so a bulk buyer sees available colourways at a glance, which is exactly the information they want.
- Several include the **branded ZONIXA poly-bag packaging**, printed with wash-care symbols and "A Product From Panwar Knitwear, Ludhiana (India)" — genuine private-label proof.
- Garments show real **embroidered logos, woven labels, hang tags and chest prints** — direct evidence of the branding services we want to sell.

**The background is a dark charcoal textured floor, and it is consistent across all 29 shots.**

> **Design decision:** do not fight this background — build with it. Charcoal sits naturally against the ink `#14131A` palette, so these photos placed in dark sections read as deliberate art direction rather than as accidental floor shots. Cropping them onto fake white backgrounds would look worse, not better.

## What to ask the client for

1. **MSP Sports product photos** — half the product range currently has no imagery at all.
2. **Factory photos** — even phone photos of the knitting machines, stitching line and packing area would transform the credibility of the site. This is the single highest-value asset they could supply.
3. **A real email address.**
4. Fabric close-ups — a phone macro of each of the 8 fabrics.
5. A photo of the team or the founders on the factory floor.
